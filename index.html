What Is This Website?

The Internship & Career Guide is a free resource built for university students who want a clear, step-by-step path from choosing a career to landing an internship.

Who this is For?

University students in any year who are starting to think about careers, internships, and life after graduation.

What do we do?

Guides you through five stages: explore careers, build skills, prepare your CV, practice interviews, and apply for internships.

Where Do I Start?

Begin with the Career Guide to understand your options, then move through each stage at your own pace.
