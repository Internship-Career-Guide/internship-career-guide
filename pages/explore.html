Learn how to explore career options, understand the job market, and make confident decisions about your future.

Step 1 - Explore

Why Career Exploration Matters?

As a university student, choosing a career can feel overwhelming. The good news is that career exploration is a process, so you do not need to have all the answers right away.

Research shows that students who actively explore careers are more likely to find fulfilling jobs after graduation. This guide will walk you through a proven framework for career exploration, step by step.

Step 3 - Research

How to Research Careers

Follow these steps to learn more about your career choices.

01

Search Online

Explore career websites, job platforms, company websites, and professional resources.

02

Talk to Professionals

Interview people who already work in the career you are interested in.

03

Compare Requirements

Compare education, skills, salary, responsibilities, and career growth.

04

Make a Career Plan

Create an action plan with skills, education, projects, internships, and goals.
